Innovative learning models

An invited session in Indonesia on teacher development and innovative, effective learning models.

Role / Narasumber / Keynote speaker15 January 2026 / UIN Sultanah Nahrasiyah Lhokseumawe

The invitation

I was invited as a narasumber for a session with the postgraduate community at Universitas Islam Negeri Sultanah Nahrasiyah Lhokseumawe, connected with the Indonesian Ministry of Religious Affairs. The topic brought teacher development together with the implementation of innovative and effective learning models.

The perspective I shared

The session connects with my work in language education, design thinking and digital learning. Its place in this portfolio is the exchange of teaching practice across institutions, not a claim of measured impact that has yet to be documented.
