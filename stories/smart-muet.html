01 / Digital learning

Smart MUET Guide V2

A MUET learning guide built from classroom experience and shaped for practical use across all four language skills.

Role / Project leader with Angela and NurfarizahFocus / Listening · Speaking · Reading · Writing

The starting point

Years of teaching Communicative English and MUET made one need clear: learners benefit from a connected place to practise the four components and return to material at their own pace. Smart MUET Guide V2 is my response to that teaching need.

What I developed

The guide brings listening, speaking, reading and writing practice into one digital experience. I lead the work with Angela and Nurfarizah, connecting subject knowledge, learning activities and the app experience.

The work is iterative. We review content and usability, collect feedback, then refine the parts that cause confusion or slow learners down.

Where it is now

The app is live for review. Lecturer and teacher feedback is being sought before the planned student testing. Those responses will inform the next revision; I am not presenting unverified learning gains as an outcome.

Evidence & next steps.

Project records, feedback and later testing findings will be linked here as they are documented.
